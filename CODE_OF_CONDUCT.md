# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want tMinus to be a project anyone can contribute to, regardless of experience, background or
identity. Everyone taking part is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that a "no" is about scope and cost, not about you.
- Respect that this is volunteer work done in people's spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/saarhaber/Tminus/security/advisories/new) or by
email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this document, and may block repeat
offenders from the project.
